**Role:** BSc curricular internship (Erasmus+, 2021), KU Leuven, Belgium. Supervisors: Vera van Noort and Diksha Bhalla.

DARTpaths is an in silico platform for investigating the molecular mechanisms of compounds. It is part of the project "Development of In silico Strategies To Advance the 3Rs in Toxicity Testing" at KU Leuven.

**My contribution:** I added *Drosophila melanogaster* to the platform.
