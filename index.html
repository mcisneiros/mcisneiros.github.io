About me

I'm a bioinformatician and pre-doctoral research fellow in the Machine Learning and Knowledge Discovery group at INESC-ID, supervised by Prof. Ana Teresa Freitas. I work on MALDIBANK, a Horizon Europe project that applies machine learning to MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry for microbial identification and antibiotic resistance monitoring.

I hold a BSc in Bioinformatics (Polytechnic of Setúbal) and an MSc in Evolutionary and Developmental Biology (University of Lisbon), with a thesis at the Gulbenkian Science Institute on the comparative demography of endangered species. Before INESC-ID I was a Data Steward and Bioinformatics Technician at BioData.pt (ELIXIR Portugal), working on FAIR data, GDPR and the FEGA Portugal node for sensitive data, and a research fellow on soil ontologies in the PHENET project at ITQB NOVA. I also did an Erasmus+ traineeship at KU Leuven on in silico alternatives to animal toxicity testing, and I am a Fulbright SUSI alumnus.
