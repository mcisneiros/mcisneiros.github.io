About

I'm a bioinformatician and research fellow at INESC-ID, Instituto Superior Técnico (University of Lisbon), in the Machine Learning and Knowledge Discovery group, supervised by Prof. Ana Teresa Freitas. I work on MALDIBANK, a European Commission funded project that combines microbial mass spectrometry and AI, and I develop MaldiQC, a quality control pipeline for MALDI-TOF data.

Before that I worked in research data management at BioData.pt (ELIXIR Portugal), on FAIR data, metadata and sensitive-data governance in FEGA Portugal, and on soil ontologies in the PHENET project. My MSc, in evolutionary and developmental biology at the University of Lisbon, ended with a thesis on demographic inference in mouse lemurs at the Gulbenkian Science Institute.

Interests: AI for mass spectrometry, FAIR data and ontologies, evolutionary and population genomics.
