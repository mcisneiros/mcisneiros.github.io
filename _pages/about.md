I am a bioinformatician and research data steward based in Lisbon, Portugal. I am currently a pre-doctoral researcher in the Machine Learning and Knowledge Discovery (MLKD) group at INESC-ID / Instituto Superior Técnico, working on the MALDIBANK Horizon Europe project.

Before that, I worked as a data steward at BioData.pt (ELIXIR Portugal) on FAIR data, GDPR compliance, metadata and standards, and in research on soil ontologies (PHENET) and the comparative demography of endangered species.
