I'm a pre-doctoral researcher at INESC-ID / Instituto Superior Técnico (University of Lisbon), in the Machine Learning and Knowledge Discovery group. I work on [MALDIBANK]({{ "/projects/1_maldibank/" | relative_url }}), a Horizon Europe project applying AI to MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry for microbial identification and antibiotic resistance monitoring.

My background is in bioinformatics (BSc, Polytechnic of Setúbal) and evolutionary developmental biology (MSc, University of Lisbon), plus research data management at BioData.pt (ELIXIR Portugal), where I worked on FAIR data, metadata and sensitive-data governance. Based in Lisbon.

See my [CV]({{ "/cv/" | relative_url }}), [projects]({{ "/projects/" | relative_url }}) and [publications]({{ "/publications/" | relative_url }}).

## Research interests

- AI and machine learning for MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry
- Spectra preprocessing and quality control
- FAIR data, metadata standards and ontologies
- Sensitive data governance (GDPR, ELSI)
- Population genomics of endangered species
